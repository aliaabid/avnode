Partnership Process
01

Site identification & qualification

AVNode identifies properties near Live Moove's operational zones and qualifies them against access, power, staffing, and security criteria.

02

Network integration

Qualified sites are onboarded into the AVNode network with GPS coordinates, access protocols, and staff trained to plug in vehicles.

03

Fleet dispatch routing

Live Moove vehicles are routed to the nearest available node instead of returning to a central depot — reducing mileage and downtime immediately.

04

Infrastructure grows with demand

High-utilization nodes receive DC fast charging and fleet infrastructure upgrades as data validates the location.
