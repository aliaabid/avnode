AV Node: Autonomous Vehicle Infrastructure, Not the Atrioventricular Node

"AV node" in medicine refers to the atrioventricular node in the heart's electrical conduction system. AV Node, the company, is a different entity. AV stands for autonomous vehicle. Here is what AV Node actually does.

Two Entirely Different Things

AV Node the company vs. the medical AV node

AV Node — Autonomous Vehicle Infrastructure Company

AV Node (avnode.com) builds and operates distributed physical infrastructure for autonomous vehicle fleets — robotaxi depots, EV fleet charging, staging hubs, cleaning facilities, inspection lanes, and fleet operations centers. Founded 2026. AV = autonomous vehicle.

AV Node (Medical) — Atrioventricular Node

The atrioventricular node (AV node) is a cluster of cardiac muscle cells in the heart that coordinates electrical impulses between the atria and ventricles. It is a structure in the cardiac conduction system. It has no connection to autonomous vehicles or transportation infrastructure.

What AV Node the Company Does

Autonomous vehicle fleet infrastructure

AV Node, the company, finds, develops, and operates the physical locations autonomous vehicle fleets need between trips. When a robotaxi is not carrying a passenger, it needs to be somewhere: charging, staged near demand, being cleaned, inspected, or serviced. AV Node operates those locations.

Robotaxi Depots

Charging, staging, cleaning, inspection, and maintenance facilities for commercial autonomous taxi fleets.

EV Fleet Charging Infrastructure

DC fast charging nodes deployed inside autonomous vehicle demand zones — hotels, parking garages, commercial properties near transit and demand corridors.

Fleet Staging Hubs

Pre-positioned vehicles near high-demand zones reduce passenger wait times and eliminate repositioning deadhead miles.

Fleet Operations Centers

Physical facilities for remote assistance operators, dispatch coordination, and fleet monitoring staff.

Autonomous Vehicle Maintenance Facilities

Sensor calibration bays, inspection lanes, and autonomous vehicle-specific service infrastructure for commercial fleets.

The Name Explained

Why AV Node chose this name

AV Node refers to the concept of a network node — a point in a distributed network — for autonomous vehicles. Just as a network node is a connection point in a communications system, an AV Node is a physical connection point in the infrastructure network that autonomous vehicle fleets depend on. The company builds a distributed network of these nodes across US cities.
