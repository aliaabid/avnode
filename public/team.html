Why AVNode
exists

Autonomous vehicles will not scale through software alone.

The robotaxi companies, autonomous trucking operators, and last-mile delivery fleets being built today share one structural problem: they need physical infrastructure in cities, and there is no coordinated network to provide it.

Charging. Staging. Cleaning. Inspection. Fleet recovery. Maintenance pads. These are not optional services. They are operational requirements that happen dozens of times per vehicle, per week. Right now, every fleet operator either builds expensive central depots or runs vehicles back to origin — burning miles, burning time, and scaling poorly.

The insight behind AVNode is simple: the real estate already exists. Hotels, parking structures, mixed-use developments, and commercial properties across every city have underutilized space, power access, and staff on site. They just have no relationship with the fleet operators who need them.

AVNode was built to be the network layer between property owners who have space and fleet operators who need distributed infrastructure — qualified, connected, and managed at city scale.
