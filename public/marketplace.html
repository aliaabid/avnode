Infrastructure Categories

What AVNode Sources and Operates

Physical infrastructure for every stage of autonomous fleet operations — sourced from existing commercial real estate and activated for fleet use.

01
Charging Depots
Infrastructure capable of supporting fleet charging operations at the power levels autonomous fleets require. Sites with existing utility access or viable upgrade paths, positioned within or adjacent to active demand zones.
02
Fleet Staging
Locations positioned near demand centers to reduce deadhead miles and improve fleet utilization. Vehicles stage at these sites between high-demand windows rather than returning to a central depot.
03
Maintenance Facilities
Sites equipped to support vehicle inspection, servicing, cleaning, and operational readiness. Includes AV-specific requirements: sensor calibration zones, EV service infrastructure, and OTA update connectivity.
04
Fleet Operations Centers
Physical infrastructure for dispatch, monitoring, workforce management, and autonomous fleet support. Remote assistance operator workstations, connectivity infrastructure, and operational coordination facilities.
Built for Fleet Operators

Most infrastructure was not designed for autonomous fleets

Commercial real estate was built for human-operated vehicles, retail tenants, and office occupants. Autonomous fleets have fundamentally different requirements — power capacity, traffic flow, access schedules, security, and operational support that standard properties cannot provide.

AVNode bridges the gap between what exists in the commercial real estate market and what autonomous fleet operators actually need.

How It Works

How Deployment Works

AVNode compresses a 12–24 month infrastructure buildout into a 30–90 day activation process.

01
Submit Deployment Requirements
Fleet operator submits market, vehicle count, power requirements, infrastructure type, and timeline.
02
AVNode Identifies Qualified Infrastructure
AVNode matches requirements against pre-qualified property partners across target markets.
03
Site Diligence and Utility Review
Power capacity, access, security, and lease terms are confirmed. Utility upgrade plan is developed.
04
Infrastructure Activation
Charging hardware is deployed, utility upgrades are completed, and operations are stood up.
05
Fleet Launch
Fleet operator vehicles enter service. AVNode manages ongoing site operations under SLA.
